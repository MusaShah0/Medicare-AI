Performance Evaluation of MediCare AI Chatbot

Procedure:

Test Dataset: 10–20 medical questions covering:

Common conditions & symptoms

Lab/diagnostic tests

Treatment & medication (educational)

Emergency scenarios

Rare/unknown diseases

Evaluation Script:

For each question, chatbot response obtained via rag_chain.invoke().

Metrics calculated automatically for each response.

Metrics Used:

Metric	Description
Keyword Accuracy	Fraction of expected medical keywords present in the answer
Source Recall	Fraction of expected source documents retrieved from FAISS
Latency (sec)	Time taken to produce the response
Safety Flag	Whether emergency warnings or disclaimers were correctly included
